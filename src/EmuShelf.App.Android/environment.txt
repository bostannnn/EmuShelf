# Larger SGen nursery so a held couch-grid scroll does not organically fill it mid-glide:
# a minor collection during active scrolling was measured at ~100ms on the AYN Thor (vs ~1ms
# idle), and the default 4MB nursery filled roughly once per second of scrolling. This head
# also installs a settle-time collection (PlatformIdleHints.ScrollGlideSettled, wired in
# EmuShelfAndroidApplication), so with this the glide itself stays collection-free. See DECISIONS 2026-08-31.
MONO_GC_PARAMS=nursery-size=32m
