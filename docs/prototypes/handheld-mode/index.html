Handheld mode

A third EmuShelf interface for dual-screen Android handhelds. You pick on the bottom screen, and the top screen shows what you picked, big. Drive it with the device buttons, by tapping the bottom screen, or from the keyboard.

Drawn to the AYN Thor's screen proportions: top 1920 × 1080, bottom 1240 × 1080.

Folder icon options

Four directions, none of them using console art. The big folder shows how it looks on the top screen; the small ones are bottom-screen size, including one of your own folders (Favorites).
