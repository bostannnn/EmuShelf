PS2 and 3DS animated backdrops

Five per platform, same approach as the SNES/PS1 motion lab: no scenery, all drawn in code, and each one leans on how that console drew things. PS2 renders soft at 480×270 with smooth shading, glow, see-through layers and interlacing. 3DS renders crisp at the 3DS top-screen density with no anti-aliasing, cel shading, outlines and stereoscopic depth.
