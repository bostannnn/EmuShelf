Screen 1 of 4 · options 1 to 3

All apps

Every app on the Thor. You open it from the dock, at home or in a game. While a game runs the game has the top screen, so the list lives on the bottom screen alone.

What it holds today

  • A title: “All apps”. When you are changing a dock slot it reads “Choose an app for slot 2”.
  • Every app with its icon and name, A to Z, five across, four rows at a time.
  • A opens the app. Y pins it to the first free dock slot. B closes.
  • After a pin a line says where it went, or that the dock is full.
  • When changing a slot: A pins here, Y clears the slot.
  • By touch: tap an app to open it, hold a finger on it to pin it, drag to scroll, tap the close button.

The three options draw here.

What changes from today

  • No white screen, no round close button, no hint pills, no name under every icon. The list sits on the console’s colour, dark while a game runs.
  • The line after a pin is plain type that rolls in.
  • The icons here are stand-ins. The app shows each app’s own icon.
  • Each picture steps through: opening, moving, pinning, changing a dock slot, closing, and the list opened from home.

Every achievement

The full set of the running game’s achievements. A on the Achievements tab opens it. The game has the top screen.

What it holds today

  • “Achievements” and a count with points: “5 / 12 · 60 pts”.
  • All 12, Locked 7, Unlocked 5. LB and RB switch between them.
  • Every badge, seven across. Locked ones are dimmed. A hardcore unlock has a gold edge. A hidden one reads “Hidden achievement” until A reveals it.
  • The focused badge’s name, what it asks, its points and the day it was earned.
  • Y refreshes. A line says what a refresh is doing or why it could not. B closes.
  • By touch: tap a badge, tap a filter, tap Refresh, tap the close button, drag to scroll.

The three options draw here.

What changes from today

  • No pills, no round buttons, no strip with a line over it. The filters are three plain words.
  • The focused achievement’s name lands letter by letter. The count rolls.
  • When a refresh finds a new unlock, its badge takes its colour and the count and the points roll.
  • The badges here are stand-ins. The app shows each achievement’s own picture.
  • Each picture steps through: opening, moving to a locked one, showing only the locked, a hidden one, revealing it, a refresh, closing.

The photo viewer

A on the Photos tab opens the running game’s pictures one at a time: the screenshots first, then the video and the title screen. The game has the top screen.

What it holds today

  • The picture, fitted whole, filling the screen. A dark band at the top holds the game’s title and a round close button. A dark band at the bottom holds “1 / 6 · Screenshot” and the hints.
  • Left and right page through the pictures and go round at the ends.
  • The video shows a still with a play mark. A plays it muted and A again stops it. It only downloads when you play it.
  • Y fetches missing artwork. B closes, and the Photos tab lands on the picture you were on.
  • A line in the middle says “Loading…”, what a fetch found, or why a picture could not open.
  • By touch: swipe sideways to page, swipe down or tap the close button to close, tap the play mark, tap the picture to hide the type.

The three options draw here.

What changes from today

  • No dark bands, no round buttons. The type sits on the game’s dark colour and the count is a figure that rolls.
  • The picture grows out of its small picture on the Photos tab, and goes back into it when you close.
  • The pictures here are stand-ins. The app shows the game’s own screenshots and video.
  • Each picture steps through: opening, two pictures on, the video, playing it, a tap that hides the type, closing.

Screen 4 of 4 · options 10 to 12

The idle artwork

At home, after 15 seconds without a press, the top screen walks through the games you played last, each with its artwork. The bottom screen does not change.

What it holds today

  • It starts after 15 seconds without a press or a touch, only at home with nothing open, and never when Reduce motion or battery saver is on.
  • It walks the last twelve games you played, newest first, six seconds each, once round, then rests on the newest.
  • For each game: its artwork filling the screen, “Recently played”, its name (or its logo picture if it has one), its achievements, its console, its hours and when you last played it.
  • A game with no artwork shows its cover on a tint.
  • The clock and the battery stay. There are no button hints.
  • Any press or touch ends it and the top screen goes back to what is focused. A, B, X and Y only wake it. A direction wakes it and moves.

The three options draw here.

What changes from today

  • The name is in the big type and its letters land. The facts are the two lines under it, as when you browse.
  • A game changes through a circle from the hinge, as a console’s backdrop does. Today it is a slow fade.
  • A message from the app, such as a scan finishing, takes the line under the name. Today it is a pill over the artwork.
  • The artwork here is a stand-in painted for this page. The app shows each game’s own artwork.
  • Each picture steps through: browsing, the artwork arriving, two more games, and a press that wakes it.
