The model view, on LB and RB

PlayStation 2
76%18:00

This is the top screen only; the bottom screen does not change. E or Q is RB or LB, the arrow keys change game, and you can drag the model to turn it.

What changes

  • Today a game shows its big name with the 3D case squeezed beside it.
  • New the two are separate views. The name view is the big name alone. LB or RB switches to the model view: the model alone in the middle of the screen, no big name.
  • In the model view the game's name is only the small line at the bottom left. Say so if you want that gone too.
  • The model rises through the hinge, floats, and every 15 seconds turns once all the way round so the back and the spine show. The right stick turns it; let go and it swings back.
  • Moving to another game flips the model over into the next one. The view you chose stays as you browse.
  • The model drawn here is a stand-in box. The app uses its real models: keep case, jewel case, cartridges and cards.
