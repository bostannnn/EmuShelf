Every animation, console by console. On the left it is as it is today; on the right is what I propose. Under each, pick Leave it, Recolour or Change it. The strip under a picture is the console's colour on the bottom screen.

Recolour keeps the animation's shapes, motion and light and dark, and moves its colour to the console's. Change it means you do not want that animation, recoloured or not: say what you would rather see in the note and I draw a new one for that place. Where a console has few animations I also propose ones to add. Sharpness is a separate question on the decisions page.
